# Privacy Policy Draft

This is a starter draft for review by the church and legal/compliance advisors before publication.

## Shekinah Sons Global Privacy Policy

Shekinah Sons Global provides this mobile app to help members and visitors access sermons, devotions, Bible reading, live services, events, giving, prayer requests, and church updates.

## Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as name, email address, phone number, profile photo, city, and country when a user creates an account or updates their profile.

We may collect app activity such as saved devotions, liked sermons, watch/listen progress, Bible bookmarks, Bible highlights, downloaded Bible versions, prayer requests, giving records, and notification preferences.

For giving, payment details are processed through payment providers such as M-Pesa and any enabled card processor. The app stores giving records such as amount, category, payment method, transaction reference, and date.

For notifications, we may store device push tokens, device platform, app version, and notification preferences.

## How We Use Information

We use information to provide app features, personalize the member experience, maintain account access, process giving, send requested notifications, show saved content, and improve church communication.

## Public Prayer Requests

Prayer requests marked public may be visible to other app users. Prayer requests marked private should only be visible to the signed-in member and authorized church administrators.

## Sharing Information

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the app, including hosting, media storage, payment processing, notifications, analytics, and maps. We do not sell personal information.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

## User Choices

Users can update profile details, notification preferences, saved items, and other account settings in the app. Users may contact the church to request account or data support.
