## Full Description

The Shekinah Sons Global app helps members and friends stay connected to church life wherever they are.

Use the app to watch and listen to sermons, read daily devotions, follow live services, browse upcoming events, access Bible reading, connect with church branches, give securely, and receive important updates.

Key features:

- Video and audio sermons.
- Daily devotions and Scripture of the Day.
- Live service access.
- Offline-capable Bible reader.
- Church events and branch locations.
- Giving through supported payment methods.
- Prayer wall and prayer requests.
- Push notifications for important updates.
